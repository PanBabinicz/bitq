## SAP-1 Architecture

**The SAP (Simple-As-Possible) computer has been designed for you, the beginner.
The main purpose of SAP is to introduce all the crucial ideas behind computer
operation without burying you in unnecessary detail. But even a simple computer
like SAP covers many advanced concepts. To avoid bombarding you with too much
all at once, we will examine three different generations of the SAP computer.**
